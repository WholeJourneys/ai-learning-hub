Low-risk exercises

These prompts help you understand what a connection can see without asking it to change anything.

Google Drive: “Find the newest visitor FAQ. Summarize it and cite the file. Do not edit anything.”
Gmail: “Find messages from this sender from the last 30 days. Summarize open questions. Do not draft or send.”
Dropbox: “Find three files about this itinerary. Tell me their names, dates, and likely purpose. Do not move or delete.”
Calendar: “Show appointments next week and identify open two-hour blocks. Do not create or change events.”
Keep private information out of experiments. Do not connect financial, medical, password, or private customer information simply to see what happens.
