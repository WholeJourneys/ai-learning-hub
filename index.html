Update Protocol

Run quarterly. Discover → vet → prune → log. Copy any prompt straight into Perplexity.

Step 1 — Discover new sources

Step 2 — Vet candidates (must pass 4 of 5)

  • Free tier with real substance, not a teaser
  • Updated within the last 90 days
  • Verifiable author credentials (frontier lab, university, practitioner, established journalist)
  • Cites primary sources / original analysis, not press-release rehash
  • Recommended by at least two independent communities or curated lists

Step 3 — Prune

Remove sources that went quiet for 6+ months, moved fully behind a paywall, or shifted to low-signal promotional content.
